## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Select a Meme Template

Choose one of three ways to get started:

#### 📤 Upload Your Own
- **Desktop:** Click the **Upload** button, or **drag and drop** a supported image file directly onto the page.
- **Mobile:** Tap the **+** button to upload an image from your device.

#### 🖼️ Browse the Meme Library
- Don't have a template? Browse our curated library of the most popular memes, refreshed every **30 days** to stay relevant.
- Click any meme to open it in the editor and start customizing.

#### 🤖 AI Mode
- No idea where to start? Click the **AI Mode** button to enable it.
- Fill out the prompts describing your situation, and add your [free Gemini API key](https://aistudio.google.com/app/apikey).
- Click **Generate** to have the AI agent populate meme ideas for you.

---

## ✏️ Edit Your Meme

Once you've selected an image, you can freely customize it:

- Add your own **captions** anywhere on the image
- Adjust caption **position**, **size**, and **font style**
- If AI Mode was used, you can also **edit the AI-generated captions** directly

---

## 💾 Save & Share

### 📥 Download / Share
- **Desktop:** Click **Download** to save your final meme to your device.
- **Mobile:** Tap the **Share** button to download or share your meme however you'd like.

### 📋 Copy to Clipboard
- Available on **all devices** — click the **clipboard button** in the top-right corner of the meme editor.
- Instantly paste your meme into any group chat, social media platform, or app — no download needed.
